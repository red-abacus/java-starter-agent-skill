# Progressive Disclosure Refactor

The owner approved restructuring the portable kit before the final interview round. Keep the three workflow skills, make the handbook a short task router, and move accepted policies into focused shared references. No new engineering preferences are introduced.

## Agreed approach

Keep the 200–400 word AGENTS.md as the essential entry point. Keep bootstrap, change, and review as the discoverable workflows. Split the handbook by architecture/Java style, REST, DTO/mapping, persistence, security/errors, integrations/background work, testing, build/configuration, operations, and GKE delivery. Give references explicit read conditions; large references also provide section navigation.

Separate generic collaboration from conditional infrastructure discovery. Keep detailed policies authoritative in the topic references, with short reminders at entry points. Split the template adoption guide into task-specific recipes and update the documented portable-copy layout.

The rejected alternatives are retaining one large handbook (weak reading boundaries) and adding a skill for every topic (unnecessary discovery overhead and overlapping triggers).

## Constraints

- Preserve all accepted policies, including implementation discussion for availability controls, workflow-specific caching, deletion clarification, and external-effect ordering.
- Keep relative references portable when the entire documented kit layout is copied.
- Preserve historical verification evidence; record new checks separately and honestly.
- Do not create an application, select new products/versions, deploy, initialize Git, commit, or change unrelated files.
- Maintain optional verification-fixture links for adoption copies without the fixture.

## Verification

Record pre-refactor reading behavior for representative PATCH and minimal-bootstrap tasks. Re-run them independently after restructuring, plus a delivery-only review scenario. Inspect read inventories and decisions rather than asserting a hard token guarantee.

Extend the validator to cover the new reference tree and Markdown section destinations, with negative tests. Verify full/runtime relocated copies, skill metadata, and preservation of the original handbook's policy paragraphs. Run the kit's Python and Java checks and one independent final review.
